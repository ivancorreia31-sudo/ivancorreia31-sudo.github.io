Build 51: better-looking cars, real reflections, sharper on phones

- The simple cars outside (service van, yard, car park, showroom, street) use the workshop car's fine body skin close
  up (the coarse one only far off), so a van's rear-door and cab windows match the workshop van; their lamps are the
  workshop sizes and are kept in from the corners the same way (conformLamps24), so the yard van's tail lamps no longer
  wrap round its rear corners. Close up they also get wipers and the shark-fin aerial.
- Bodies close at the nose and tail in a superellipse instead of an ellipse (a flatter face with tighter edges) and the
  shoulder line is tighter (8.5 cm radius → 5 cm).
- Reflection probes: the workshop and the yard each capture a cube of themselves (the car left out), PMREM it and use
  it as the scene environment, so paint, glass, chrome and the floor reflect the real surroundings. Retaken when the
  garage's look changes and when the light outside changes. Captured tone-mapped in sRGB, so no shader rebuild for it.
- Resolution: when frames are slow it now stops at one screen pixel (was 0.75, very soft on phones, and battery saver
  always went there) and drops the glow pass instead; the still workshop picture respects the battery-saver cap.
- Tests: b51 (lamp reach on all 69 styles, near/far detail, both probes)
