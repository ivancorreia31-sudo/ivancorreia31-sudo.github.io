Build 53: workshop lights, a quilted bonnet pad, clad buildings over the road

- Workshop: three roof fittings (over the bay and either side) are point lights (0.95, 16 m, no shadows; off in battery
  saver via prefs34), the overhead directional light 1.3 → 0.9, and the bare concrete's roughness multiplier 1 → 0.72
  (envMapIntensity 0.8), so the floor catches the lights and highlights move over the paint.
- The bonnet's sound-deadening pad is quilted dark grey felt (canvas map + light bump, UVs flat over the pad) instead of
  plain black.
- The units over the road and the farm barn are profiled steel cladding (TX.clad / TX.cladB, faces mapped 2 m × 1 m by
  cladBox53) in their colours, with a blockwork plinth; the painted shop fronts are drawn at 1024×512 with lit ribs, a
  plinth and dirt.
- skin24 faceX inverts the superellipse ends from Build 51 (it still assumed ellipses).
- Tests: b53 (pad, lights and battery saver, cladding)
