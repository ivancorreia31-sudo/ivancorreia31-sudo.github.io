Bonnet Up privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Bonnet Up is a single-player car mechanic game. You don’t need an account to play, and the game doesn’t collect, store or share any personal information about you.

What the game keeps

Your progress (your garage, cash, level and the cars you’re working on) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves your device unless you copy a save code yourself with “Move your save”. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes your progress.

What the game downloads

The first time it runs, the game downloads its 3D engine (three.js, from the cdnjs network) and its fonts (from Google Fonts). As with any web request, those services can see your device’s IP address. After that, the game keeps its own copy and works offline.

What the game doesn’t do

  • No adverts.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.

Children

The game doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, email the address shown on the Bonnet Up page in Google Play.
